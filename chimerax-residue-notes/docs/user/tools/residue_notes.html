Residue Notes

The Residue Notes tool is a ChimeraX interface for reading, browsing, editing, and exporting residue annotations embedded in mmCIF files using the custom _cootnote_residue_note.* loop written by coot_trimmings.

Notes are grouped by residue, single-chain region, or Cartesian position and can be navigated with the thread list or the previous/next buttons. Export writes an annotated mmCIF by saving the current model as mmCIF and then injecting the custom annotation loop. Navigation preserves the current center-of-rotation mode and pivot marker settings, including those set with cofron.

Choose Add note at center of rotation to annotate a point without a residue. This captures the existing CoFR when the editor opens, not the view centre; there are no editable coordinate fields. Clicking a positional thread centres it without changing zoom or orientation and shows a temporary cyan sphere. The point is independent of residue edits and numbering. Coordinates use the associated model's file-coordinate frame, follow rigid model transforms in ChimeraX, and round-trip with Coot via the target_kind=position and position_x/y/z fields.

Select residues and choose Add note from selection to annotate a residue or a contiguous single-chain region directly. Disjoint selections and selections spanning chains or models are not silently expanded. Alternatively, Choose range... accepts a chain, start, and end, or Pick endpoints lets you click the first and last residues in the 3D view. Dragging still rotates; Cancel picking exits without adding a note. Cyan and orange spheres mark the endpoints; they disappear when finished or cancelled and are never exported as atoms. The previous mouse binding is restored afterwards. Insertion codes and numbering gaps are supported. A title alone is enough for a short region label. Clicking its thread navigates to and selects the entire range in either notes pane, without changing atom/ribbon colours or visibility. The centre follows the current range atoms, including after model transformations or annotated mmCIF reloads.

Range and positional navigation sets a symmetric, camera-aligned clipping slab centred on the target, at least 10 Å deep (5 Å on each side). Larger ranges get at least 10 Å of depth context beyond their atoms on each side. Old scene-fixed front/back planes are replaced by the new near/far slab, while the current center-of-rotation mode and pivot marker settings are preserved.

The nearby_notes command opens a read-only browser around the centre of rotation. A region is nearby when any member residue falls within the selected radius. Positional notes use their point's distance from CoFR; clicking one navigates to it. Both panes share edits immediately; saving annotated mmCIF or Markdown remains explicit.

Preserving notes: export annotated mmCIF to retain editable notes with the model, or export Markdown for a report. Ordinary model saving and ChimeraX session saving do not preserve new notes. Gemmi and a Coot installation are not required to use this bundle.
